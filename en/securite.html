🔒 Security

Your data's security, by design

BD Report protects your commercial information with industry standards: hosting in the European Union, encryption in transit and at rest, strict data separation and GDPR compliance.

This page describes the technical and organisational measures in place to ensure the confidentiality, integrity and availability of your data.

🛡️ Data security

Protecting your data sits at the core of BD Report. We apply industry best practice to guarantee its confidentiality, integrity and availability.

No data is sold, shared or exploited for commercial purposes. Your data belongs to you and only serves to run the service for you.

🏛️ Hosting and infrastructure

Your data is hosted within the European Union, on Supabase infrastructure operated in professional data centres, with network isolation and continuous monitoring.

🇪🇺 EU hosting 24/7 monitoring Network isolation

🔐 Encryption

All data is encrypted at rest with AES‑256, and in transit over HTTPS / TLS 1.2 or above. Exchanges between your browser and our servers are always secured.

AES‑256 at rest TLS 1.2+ in transit

👤 Access and permissions

Access is controlled by role — Founder, Manager, Member, Support — with detailed per-feature permissions, and every workspace can be protected by an access code.

  • Each role only sees the screens and data its permissions allow.
  • Personal workspaces are separated by company and by teammate.
  • Every action is timestamped in an audit log you can read and export.
Where we actually stand, plainly: separation is currently enforced by the application, and data is encrypted at rest. Database-level isolation (Row Level Security per organisation) is being rolled out and will be announced here once deployed. We'd rather describe what exists than what is planned.

💾 Backups and continuity

Automatic daily backups are taken to ensure service continuity and fast restoration in case of an incident.

⚖️ GDPR compliance

BD Report complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You have the following rights at any time:

  • Right of access to your personal data.
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate information.
  • Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”).
  • Right to portability of your data.

📤 Deleting and exporting your data

You can export all of your data at any time from the app (Settings → Download), in JSON format.

You can also request complete deletion of your data through our contact form or by email (see below). Your request is handled promptly.
